# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors and maintainers of Expender, we pledge to make contributions a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- **Using welcoming and inclusive language**
- **Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences**
- **Gracefully accepting constructive criticism**
- **Focusing on what is best for the project**
- **Showing empathy towards other contributors**
- **Demonstrating professionalism in all interactions**

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment, trolling, or discriminatory language**
- **Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments**
- **Public or private harassment of any kind**
- **Publishing others' private information without explicit permission**
- **Sexual attention or advances of any kind**
- **Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting**
- **Disrupting discussions or project activities**
- **Spamming or off-topic content in project spaces**

## Enforcement Responsibilities

If any member of the community violates this code of conduct, maintainers of the Expender project may take action, removing issues, comments, and PRs or blocking accounts as deemed appropriate. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Enforcement

### Reporting Guidelines

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to:

**📧 Email**: [conduct@expender.app](mailto:conduct@expender.app)

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### Reporting Process

When reporting a violation, please include:

1. **What happened**: A clear description of the incident
2. **When it happened**: Date and time (with timezone if possible)
3. **Who was involved**: Names or usernames of people involved
4. **Evidence**: Screenshots, links, or other documentation
5. **Impact**: How the incident affected you or others

### Confidentiality

All reports will be handled with discretion. We are committed to protecting the privacy of those who report violations. Details of the incident will only be shared with those directly involved in the resolution process.

## Appeals Process

If you believe you have been unfairly accused of violating this Code of Conduct, you may appeal the decision by:

1. **Contacting the enforcement team** at [conduct@expender.app](mailto:conduct@expender.app)
2. **Providing additional context** or evidence relevant to your case
3. **Requesting a review** by a different community leader if there's a conflict of interest

Appeals will be reviewed within 48 hours, and the decision will be communicated back to you.
